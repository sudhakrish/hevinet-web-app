Pepper (Black Pepper)

Bold, aromatic Malabar and Tellicherry black pepper.

Grown along India's Malabar Coast in the hill regions of Kerala and Karnataka (Wayanad, Idukki), Indian black pepper is prized worldwide for its high piperine content and intense aroma. Hevinet supplies garbled (cleaned) pepper to export-grade specifications, including the sought-after Tellicherry bold grades.

Origin regionsMalabar Coast — Wayanad and Idukki, Kerala & Karnataka
GradesMalabar Garbled (MG1) and Tellicherry Garbled Special Extra Bold (TGSEB); ungarbled available
Quality metricsPiperine content, bulk density (g/l), and moisture, tested per lot
Form suppliedWhole black peppercorns; white pepper and pepper powder on request
Typical applicationsSpice blends, food manufacturing, pharmaceutical use
