Dry Red Chilies

Vibrant colour and measured heat from Guntur and Byadgi.

India's chili-growing belt covers the full flavour and heat spectrum: Sannam (S4), valued for balanced heat and colour; hotter Teja (S17) and 334 Wonder Hot for buyers needing higher Scoville intensity; Byadgi, prized for deep red colour, mild heat, and oleoresin/colour extraction; and Kashmiri, prized for vibrant colour with very low heat. Supplied whole or stemless to export-grade cleaning standards.

Origin regionsGuntur (Andhra Pradesh), Byadgi (Karnataka), and other recognized chili-growing belts
VarietiesSannam (S4), Teja (S17), Byadgi, 334 Wonder Hot, and Kashmiri
